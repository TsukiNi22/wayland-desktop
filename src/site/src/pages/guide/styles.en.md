Every style is a page: the shared kit, its colors, its widgets and its interactions.

## System {#system}

:::html
<figure><img src="system-desktop.jpg" alt="System" loading="lazy" width="1280" height="720" style="width: 100%; height: auto; border-radius: 8px"></figure>
:::

- Floating pill bar (Waybar-like): workspaces = scenes, calendar on the clock, live CPU / memory / network.
- Live system monitor: a click on a process ends it.
- Quick settings that work: dark / light style, night light, do not disturb, tiling, brightness, power profile.

Open: [system.html](https://tsukini22.github.io/wayland-desktop/poc/system.html)

## Dev / low level {#dev}

:::html
<figure><img src="dev-desktop.jpg" alt="Dev / low level" loading="lazy" width="1280" height="720" style="width: 100%; height: auto; border-radius: 8px"></figure>
:::

- A playable debugger: step (`si`), continue, breakpoints (click a line), registers and flags that change, memory and stack.
- The program really prints its `printf` in the stdout window; hex dump wallpaper, binary clock, amber CRT wallpaper.
- BIOS-like lock screen.

Open: [dev.html](https://tsukini22.github.io/wayland-desktop/poc/dev.html)

## Ado / blue rose {#jpop}

:::html
<figure><img src="jpop-desktop.jpg" alt="Ado / blue rose" loading="lazy" width="1280" height="720" style="width: 100%; height: auto; border-radius: 8px"></figure>
:::

- Ado Box: a backlit cage on a marbled red / blue wall, the silhouette bends backward with the music.
- Ado TV: the official MV, Uta songs, 歌いました covers, live footage and streams of her channel, the live now, other platforms.
- 3D blue roses falling (click one), うっせぇわ stamp, wishes that bloom, timeline, stickers, Uta theme.

Open: [jpop.html](https://tsukini22.github.io/wayland-desktop/poc/jpop.html)

## Terminal / better-tty {#tty}

:::html
<figure><img src="tty-desktop.jpg" alt="Terminal / better-tty" loading="lazy" width="1280" height="720" style="width: 100%; height: auto; border-radius: 8px"></figure>
:::

- No desktop: tmux-like tiling, <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>b</kbd> then arrows / `c` / `x` / `z` / `d` / a digit, EDIT / CONTROL mode in the status bar.
- getty login as lock screen, w3m-like browser, `git`, `cmake` (animated build), `nvim`.
- btop and calendar panes, rose-pine colors like the tmux configuration.

Open: [tty.html](https://tsukini22.github.io/wayland-desktop/poc/tty.html)

## Miku {#miku}

:::html
<figure><img src="miku-desktop.jpg" alt="Miku" loading="lazy" width="1280" height="720" style="width: 100%; height: auto; border-radius: 8px"></figure>
:::

- The official KEI art (or an animated drawing in the settings) hops on the beat and sings the notes.
- Vocal editor: draw a melody on the grid, she sings it; BPM and wave.
- Hold the beam button to charge it, release: MIKU MIKU BEAM, leek rain at full power.

Open: [miku.html](https://tsukini22.github.io/wayland-desktop/poc/miku.html)

## Photographer {#photographer}

:::html
<figure><img src="photographer-desktop.jpg" alt="Photographer" loading="lazy" width="1280" height="720" style="width: 100%; height: auto; border-radius: 8px"></figure>
:::

- Photo wall left and right, film strip: a click shows a photo, the lightbox browses them (arrows, <kbd>Esc</kbd>).
- Real RGB histogram computed from the shown photo, slideshow, black & white mode.
- Viewfinder lock screen: rule of thirds, focus brackets, shooting settings.

Open: [photographer.html](https://tsukini22.github.io/wayland-desktop/poc/photographer.html)
