The photographer page loads placeholder photos from [picsum.photos](https://picsum.photos) (Unsplash license) at run time.
The Ado page uses no official Ado artwork (none is under a free license): only blue rose photos, a drawn silhouette and facts
(song titles, dates) from public sources. All the music is generated live by the kit synth (original melodies, no samples).

The Ado page lists official videos of Ado's YouTube channel (titles, ids, thumbnails loaded from YouTube) and plays them with the
YouTube embedded player: nothing is downloaded or stored here. YouTube refuses embeds on `file://` pages, so run `./serve.sh jpop.html`
to watch them inside the page; on `file://` each video opens on YouTube. The Ado Box stage (cage, silhouette, marbled light wall) is drawn
in SVG, inspired by the look of her live shows.
